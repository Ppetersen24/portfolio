How one Top Ten synopsis is built

This representative reconstruction uses public sources and synthetic portfolio data. It follows the Top Ten workflow I built in 2026: each of the ten largest holdings gets its factor data pulled through SQL, published research on the same company is paired in, Claude drafts one synopsis from that material, and an analyst reviews the result. Mercedes-Benz Group AG is used because it connects to my public 2025 equity pitch. The position data, scores, and dates are synthetic. The factor names are generic placeholders. No client, portfolio, or proprietary firm information appears here.
